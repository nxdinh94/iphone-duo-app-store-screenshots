# iPhone Duo App Store Screenshots

Use [the source guide](references/sources.md) for Apple requirements, Figma frame math, and optional layout references. Verify Apple's current screenshot specifications before preparing final deliverables. Use the capture and composition steps only as needed for the requested task.

1. **Choose the targets.** Identify the app build, locales, displays, and supported orientations. For a full Duo set, cover the outer and inner displays in orientations that show the app working as intended. Treat Duo exports and standard iPhone exports as separate targets.
2. **Capture the actual app.** Use iPhone Duo hardware, the Duo simulator in Xcode Device Hub, or existing captures from either. Capture the requested display and orientation while the app is running. Use demo accounts and check for personal data. When captures are missing, obtain them from a runnable build if possible. Otherwise, identify the missing views and label any layout studies as drafts. Preserve the original captures.
3. **Compose each display independently.** Show a quick task on the outer display and a task that benefits from the extra space on the inner display when the app supports both experiences. For continuity, capture the same content before and after unfolding. Use raw captures or place them inside an opaque canvas with optional device art and a short, accurate benefit headline. Keep the interface prominent and preserve its proportions and orientation. For framed compositions, consult the [Duo device frame sheet](references/duo-device-frame-reference.png) and select the matching pose. Use the top-left device frame for Closed portrait. Size the screen content inside the device artwork from a 1398 × 2034 px base for Closed portrait (466 × 678 at 3×); this is the screen area, excluding the bezel. Use the [screen sizing rules](references/sources.md#screen-size-inside-device-artwork) for every other state and orientation. Scale the screen and matching device frame uniformly together to fit the composition; never stretch the capture to fit a generic tall phone outline. Preserve the capture’s own camera and status area. Keep the first image legible on its own at thumbnail size, including when adjacent screenshots form a pair. Keep important content clear of any illustrated fold or crop. Apple evaluates whether more than half of each device's screenshots show the app in use, including for localized sets.
4. **Export and verify.** Export each final file to the exact dimensions for its display and orientation. Use opaque RGB PNG or JPEG with no alpha channel. Inspect each file's actual format, width, height, and alpha and review the sequence at full size and thumbnail size. For framed artwork, also verify the embedded screen’s base dimensions and uniform placement scale before any perspective transform; a correctly sized export alone does not prove that the phone inside it has the right proportions. For each deliverable, record the display, orientation, locale, and order in filenames or an export list. Keep export files separate from source captures and editable artwork. Upload or submit only when the user asks for that action.
